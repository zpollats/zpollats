# Zach Pollatsek

Senior Data Engineer building healthcare data pipelines in Salt Lake City, UT.

At Medlytix, I work on the pipelines behind patient insurance discovery for hospital billing. I own the Airflow DAG architecture for our S3 → DuckLake → S3 client pipelines, design schema and indexing changes for a multi-terabyte PostgreSQL environment, and deliver data used by our reporting and AI teams.

Some of what that work has produced:

- Cut a large client's daily processing time from 4 hours to 1 by replacing four redundant database loads with a single preprocessing pipeline
- Reduced recurring vendor costs by tens of thousands of dollars a year by redesigning request logic to eliminate redundant external lookups

During that time, I also co-founded **Lumirate**, a healthcare price-transparency startup, where I worked on parsing payer machine-readable files (MRFs) of up to 120GB into ClickHouse on Google Cloud. I started my career as a mechanical engineer at Traeger Pellet Grills.
